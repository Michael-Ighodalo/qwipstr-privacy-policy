Qwipstr Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 2025
1. The Offline Promise

Qwipstr is a 100% offline AI. Unlike other assistants, Qwipstr processes all conversations, voice-to-text, and image analysis locally on your physical device. We do not own, operate, or maintain any cloud servers for your personal data. Your conversations never leave your phone.

2. Data We Collect: The Secret Diary

To provide a personalized companion experience, Qwipstr stores the following information in your phone's local memory:

  • Your name and birthday
  • Your favorite color, music, and food
  • A nickname for your bot

This data is used solely to customize chat responses and trigger local birthday reminders.

3. Senses: Camera and Mic

Microphone: Used for offline voice recognition. No audio is recorded or stored.

Camera: Used for face/smile detection and reading text. Analysis is performed instantly on-device. Qwipstr does not save these photos unless you save them to your gallery.

4. Legal and Subpoenas

Because Qwipstr is offline, the developer has no technical ability to access or retrieve your data. In the event of a legal subpoena, the developer cannot provide chat logs or user profiles because they do not exist on our systems. Data security depends entirely on your physical device security.
